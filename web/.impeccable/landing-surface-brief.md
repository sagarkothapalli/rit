# Landing surface brief

## Scope and mode

Public landing page at `app/page.tsx`. Persuade through a visible working transformation, then support reading and trust.

## Audience and job

Indian citizens who know their problem but not RTI drafting language. They must understand within seconds that this service turns natural speech or text into a records focused draft and helps identify a Central public authority.

## Primary action and truth boundary

Primary action: start drafting. The service is independent citizen assistance and does not file on a government portal. Use no “demo” wording and do not promote the statutory fee.

## Chosen direction

Approved comp: `.impeccable/mocks/landing-option-a.png`. A restrained public service workspace with a navy civic masthead, flat off white surfaces, sparse saffron and green signals, and Apple grade spacing. The first viewport visibly transforms a spoken complaint into numbered records requests. Seed key `2d3f5aee`.

## Component grammar

Corners are 6 to 12 pixels. Lines are one pixel. Shadows are rare, neutral, and softly offset. Typography is a bilingual capable public service sans with compact labels and generous body leading. Navigation is a solid masthead, controls are rectangular with small corner softening, and status is expressed through plain text plus restrained color.

## Implementation inventory

| Region | Commitment | Medium |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Masthead | Three lion emblem, bilingual identity, independent assistance label | Official raster emblem plus semantic HTML |
| Hero | 40/60 split, clear action, speech transcript becoming numbered records | Semantic HTML, CSS, authored SVG icons |
| Motion | One orchestrated conversion sequence plus varied viewport reveals | CSS and IntersectionObserver |
| Service path | Six connected citizen steps | Semantic ordered list and CSS connectors |
| RTI lifecycle | Reply, transfer, no reply, appeal and CIC branches based on RTI Online | Semantic HTML, CSS connectors, official source image link |
| Safeguards | Exemption and evidence handling in two text columns | Semantic HTML and authored SVG icons |
| Footer | Formal information architecture and official portal reference | Semantic HTML |

## Constraints

No purple, gradients, glass, pricing promotion, “demo” wording, decorative dash characters, exaggerated claims, excessive rounded cards, or misleading government affiliation. Mobile layout must preserve reading order and at least 44 pixel targets. Motion must respect reduced motion.
